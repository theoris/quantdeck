# QuantDeck Custom Indicator — No-Code Guided Prompt Templates (`prompt_indy_nocode.md`)

> **For Non-Coders / Beginners:** You do **not** need any programming knowledge. Simply attach [`custom_indy.md`](./custom_indy.md) to your AI chat (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Cursor), copy the template below in your language, and write **1–2 sentences** describing what you want to see on your chart.
>
> The AI will **analyze your idea first**, ask you **3–4 easy multiple-choice questions with smart recommendations** (no coding jargon), and then build the ready-to-use `.ts` indicator file for `custom_indicators/` once you confirm.

---

## 🇺🇸 English (EN)

```text
I do NOT know how to code. Please help me design and build a QuantDeck Custom Indicator (.ts) strictly following the attached custom_indy.md guide.

[My Idea (In Plain Words)]
- What I want the indicator to help me see or decide: <e.g., "Show me when the trend is clearly up or down, and mark good buy/sell entry points without cluttering the chart.">
- Market / Timeframe I trade most: <e.g., "Gold (XAUUSD) on M15" or "Any">

[How I Want You (the AI) to Help Me]
1. First, analyze my idea and DO NOT generate code yet.
2. Explain your plan in simple, non-technical trading terms (no programming jargon).
3. Ask me 3–4 short, easy questions with Recommended options (A / B / C) so I can choose:
   - Where to show it (on the price chart vs. a separate bottom window)
   - Visual style (lines, colored zones/boxes, buy/sell arrows, or a summary HUD panel)
   - Sensitivity / signal frequency (fast scalping vs. smooth swing trend)
   - What settings I should be able to adjust later
4. Once I reply with my choices (or say "use recommended"), generate the complete, crash-free .ts file following all technical rules in custom_indy.md.
```
